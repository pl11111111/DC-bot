Community escrow trades use **USDT on BSC / BEP20**. Funds go to the escrow account first. The seller can request payment only after the buyer confirms receipt.

### 🛒 Trading Process

**📝 1. Start a Trade**

Click **Buy** on a post tagged **sell**, or **Sell** on a post tagged **buy**. You can also right-click the other participant → Apps → Start Trade (buy/sell).

Enter the item name and price. Delivery method, deadline, and special terms are optional, but agreeing on them beforehand is recommended.

**🤝 2. Confirm the Trade**

The bot creates a private trading channel and mentions both participants. The participant who did not initiate the trade must review the details and click **Confirm Trade**.

The minimum item price is shown in the form. The current escrow fee is **2 USDT**; eligible existing credits can offset it.

The invitation must be confirmed within **30 minutes**. After confirmation, the buyer has **15 minutes** to click **Get Payment Information**. The bot sends a reminder before the deadline and ends the order if no action is taken.

**💳 3. Buyer Pays**

Only the buyer should pay the exact amount shown in the payment details.

The seller can also see those details, but must wait until the bot displays **Payment Confirmed** before delivering. A payment screenshot alone is not proof that funds have arrived.

**📦 4. Deliver and Confirm Receipt**

After delivering the item, the seller clicks **Mark as Shipped**.

Once the buyer has actually received and checked the item, they click **Confirm Receipt** and complete the second confirmation. Do not confirm early if the item has not arrived or there is a problem. Use **Open Dispute** when available to request administrator assistance.

**💰 5. Collect Payment**

The seller clicks **Collect Payment**, enters their own USDT-BEP20 receiving address, and checks the estimated amount received and network fee before confirming.

The network fee is deducted from the seller’s proceeds. The order shows **Trade Completed** after the bot verifies that the transfer succeeded.

**⏳ Cancellation and Channel Closure**

Before payment details are generated, either participant can click **Cancel Trade**. Once payment details exist or payment has been sent, follow the available buttons and administrator instructions.

Channels are normally removed about **5 minutes** after completion, cancellation, or a successful refund. Use the keep-channel button before closure if needed.

After an administrator records a manual refund, the buyer receives a separate closure notice. The buyer can confirm closure or request that the channel remain open. If there is no response within **30 minutes**, it closes automatically.

Orders with reported payment problems require administrator review. Closing a channel does not resolve outstanding funds.

The following sections explain how to prepare and pay with USDT.

### 🎒 Before You Pay

You need an exchange account or wallet that can send **USDT on BSC**, with enough funds for the payment and any transfer fees.

- **Exchange:** Lets you buy USDT using supported payment methods and send it to others. If your funds are already on an exchange, you can pay directly from there.
- **Wallet:** Lets you receive, hold, and send cryptocurrency. Creating an empty wallet does not give you USDT; you must fund it first.
- **USDT is the currency; BSC / BEP20 is the network.** Both must be correct. USDT on another network cannot be used directly for this payment.

**🪙 Don’t Have USDT Yet?**

Choose an exchange available in your region that supports USDT withdrawals on BSC. Install its app through official channels, register, and complete any required identity verification.

Use its **Buy Crypto / Buy** section to purchase USDT with a supported payment method. Then check **Assets / Wallet** to confirm that the USDT has arrived and is available to send. Menu names vary by platform.

Finish these preparations before starting a trade and generating payment details, so the order does not expire while you register or buy USDT.

**👛 Using a Personal Wallet**

Install a BSC-compatible wallet through its official channels. Create a wallet and securely store its recovery information. If it uses a recovery phrase, back it up offline and never share it.

Select BSC, open **Receive**, and copy **your own wallet address**. You can then send USDT on BSC to it from an exchange or another wallet.

Standard BSC wallet transfers also require some BNB for network fees. Check the fee shown by your wallet.

Funding your wallet and paying an order are separate actions. **Do not use the bot’s order payment address as your personal wallet address.**

### 🧾 Get Payment Details

After both participants confirm the item, price, and terms, the buyer clicks **Get Payment Information**. The bot shows:

- **Exact amount to be received:** Copy the full amount, including all **6 decimal places**.
- **Receiving address:** Use only the address shown for the current order.
- **Currency and network:** USDT · BSC / BEP20.
- **Payment deadline:** Do not send funds after it expires.

The amount includes the item price, the applicable escrow fee, and a small decimal adjustment used to identify the order.

**Do not send only the item price, round to a whole number, or shorten the amount to two decimal places.**

### 💸 Choose How to Pay

**🏦 Paying from an Exchange**

1. Open **Assets / Wallet**, select USDT, then choose **Withdraw / Send**. Here, “withdraw” means sending USDT to another address, not to a bank account.
2. Choose an address transfer, paste the order’s receiving address, and select **BSC / BEP20**.
3. Enter the amount. On the confirmation screen, check **Amount Received / Recipient Receives** and make sure it exactly matches the full order amount.
4. Check the address, network, and amount, complete the platform’s verification, and submit **once**.

If Binance identifies the transfer as internal and shows no fee, no extra amount is needed. For on-chain withdrawals or other exchanges, follow the fees and recipient amount shown on the platform.

The bot checks payments using the order address and amount. **Do not use a UID, email address, or Binance Pay instead.**

**👛 Paying from a Wallet**

1. Switch to **BSC**, select USDT, and tap **Send**.
2. Paste the order address or scan the QR code. **The QR code contains only the address; you must still enter the amount.**
3. Enter the full USDT amount and check the recipient amount and network fee.
4. Confirm and send **once**. Standard wallet transfers pay the network fee separately in BNB; do not add it to the order’s USDT amount.

If your USDT is on another network, switching the wallet’s selected network does not move it to BSC. Stop and check where your funds are before paying.

### ✅ After Payment

Return to the trading channel and wait for **Payment Confirmed**.

An exchange showing “Submitted” does not necessarily mean the escrow account has received the funds. **Do not pay again because the bot has not updated yet.**

After the bot confirms payment, the seller delivers the item. The buyer confirms receipt only after receiving and checking it, and the seller can then collect payment.

### 🆘 Payment Problems

Click **Call Administrator** in the trading channel and provide the **order ID, actual amount sent, transfer reference / transaction hash, and a payment screenshot**.

You can usually find the transaction hash in the exchange’s withdrawal history or the wallet’s transaction details.

- **Not paid yet:** Ask for help if you are unsure about the network, amount, or fee. Do not send a test payment.
- **Wrong amount:** Do not top up the difference, split the payment, or pay again. An administrator must verify receipt and manually refund it. Any refund network fee will be deducted and explained.
- **Paid but the order timed out:** Click the button indicating that you have paid and want to cancel channel closure, then wait for administrator review.
- **Wrong network or address:** Contact an administrator and the sending platform immediately. Recovery cannot be guaranteed.

🛡️ Administrators do not need your password, recovery phrase, private key, or verification code. Never share them.

**Administrators will not send links or ask you to download files, install software, or enable remote access to verify a payment, process a refund, or unfreeze funds.**

If you receive such a request, stop and verify it in the community trading channel.
