### 🛒 How to Trade
1. **Start:** Click **Buy** on a sell post or **Sell** on a buy post, or right-click a member → Apps → Start Trade (buy/sell). Enter the item and price; agree on delivery and any special terms.
2. **Confirm:** The other participant checks the order and confirms within **30 minutes**. The buyer then has **15 minutes** to get payment information. The minimum price appears in the form. The escrow fee is **2 USDT**, less eligible credits.
3. **Pay:** Only the buyer pays. The seller must wait for the bot’s **Payment Confirmed** and delivery notice. Screenshots alone do not prove payment.
4. **Deliver:** The seller delivers, then clicks **Mark as Shipped**. The buyer checks the item before **Confirm Receipt** and the second confirmation. If something is wrong, use **Open Dispute**.
5. **Collect:** The seller enters their own USDT-BEP20 address and checks the payout before confirming. Network fees are deducted from the seller’s proceeds. Completion follows transfer confirmation.

### 🎒 First Time Using USDT?
An **exchange** lets you buy and send USDT. A **wallet** starts empty and needs funding. Use official apps available in your region that support USDT-BSC, and complete required verification.

Prepare funds **before requesting payment details**. You can pay directly from an exchange. A personal BSC wallet needs USDT plus some **BNB for network fees**. Never use the order address as your own wallet’s deposit address.

### 💳 Pay the Exact Amount
Use the current order’s address and **full amount, including all 6 decimal places**. It includes the item price, applicable escrow fee and identifying decimal amount. Do not round, split payments or add extra USDT.

**Exchange:** Select USDT → Withdraw/Send → paste the order address → choose **BSC / BEP20**. The final **Recipient Receives** amount must equal the order amount. Check the platform’s fee; Binance internal transfers may be free. Do not use UID, email or Binance Pay.

**Wallet:** Select USDT on BSC → Send → paste or scan the address → enter the exact amount. **The QR code contains only the address.** Network fees are normally paid separately in BNB. Switching networks does not move your funds to BSC.

Send **once** and wait for bot confirmation. **Do not pay after expiry or pay again while waiting.**
